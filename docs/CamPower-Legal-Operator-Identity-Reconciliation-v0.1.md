## 13. Publication responsibility

Official text already read: Law No. 2010/021, article 30. If a person provides electronic-commerce services, they must give easy, direct and permanent access to their identity, establishment address, email and telephone, and, where applicable, trade-register number, capital, registered office, taxpayer number, authorisation, and professional body. Article 5 requires electronic advertising to be identifiable as such and as coming from the person for whom it is made.

That duty identifies the provider. It does not create an office called “Directeur de la publication”. That title is a common website convention, especially from French press practice. It is **not required on the evidence read** for this Site Vitrine. Do not invent the role. Counsel can still say whether some other Cameroonian press or communications rule applies. That question is legal review, not a reason to name someone now.

Whether article 30 applies to an informational site that does not itself take orders remains **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED**. The conditions reconciliation already recorded that limit.
