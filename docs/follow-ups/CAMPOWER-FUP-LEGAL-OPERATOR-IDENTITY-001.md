## Facts still required

- legal name of the operator;
- legal form;
- registration or RCCM, or a documented statement that the operator is not registered;
- NIU where it is the operator’s identifier and publication is appropriate;
- establishment address, and registered office if it differs;
- public legal telephone;
- the relationship, if any, between the operator and the Google Play display name “Hope corporation”;
- the operator contact details that are appropriate to publish, including who receives `contact@cam-power.net`.

An intended name supplied by the Product Owner is not the same as registry verification.
